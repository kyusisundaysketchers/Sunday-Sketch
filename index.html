Groups & rotation

Sequence not final

Attendees get a colour tag at registration. The colour controls where they go, so the groups never cross. Tap a colour to follow its morning.

Proposed rotation — a straight loop, not yet confirmed by the team
GroupStation 1 · 9:40Station 2 · 10:20Station 3 · 11:20

Seats in the room

24 maximum, 8 per group. The meeting first floated 8–10 per station; the later notes settle on 8.

Why colour tags

  • Each group knows its next station without being told twice.
  • Groups do not cross paths during transitions.
  • Station holders can count heads fast.
  • Tags are cheap and reusable for future events.
Still open. The meeting asked whether the first group moves to the red or the orange station next. Confirm the full rotation matrix so it can be printed on the tags and the station signs.

Marketing

How the event gets announced, in three phases.

Announcement in three phases

  1. Save the date. Lock the date in people's heads first. Around four weeks out.
  2. Model reveal. Post only after the models sign off. This is the strongest pull.
  3. Event push. Concept, stations and the registration link. Instagram, Facebook, Threads.

Paid ads stay small and targeted, with a controlled daily spend, rather than one large burst.
